Many to Many keeps element relationships editable from either side. Connect related content once, then let authors manage the association from whichever element they are already working on.
<!-- feature-intro-end -->

<!-- feature-section media-size="large" media-shadow="false" -->
## Two-way relationships

Configure a paired Craft relation field and Many to Many field. When an author changes either side, the plugin updates the same underlying Craft relation on the linked elements. Entries, categories, users and assets can participate, with their section, group or volume keeping selections focused.

![An ingredient entry connected to two related recipes.](../screenshots/many-to-many-source.png)

<!-- feature-section-end -->

<!-- feature-section media-size="large" media-shadow="false" -->
## Native Craft relations

The field uses Craft’s element-selection patterns and stores a Craft relation, so templates, element queries and GraphQL can work with selected entries, categories, users and assets using established Craft patterns.
